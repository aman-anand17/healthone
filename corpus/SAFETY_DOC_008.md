## 1. Why we are revising

In March of this year the Committee issued SAFETY_DOC_007, which required
serum potassium to be checked at least every three months for patients
receiving CardioZen with a diagnosis of chronic kidney disease. Five months of
operating experience is now available.

Two findings prompt this revision. First, among patients who did develop
clinically significant potassium elevation while compliant with the
three-month interval, the elevation was in most cases detectable earlier than
the next scheduled measurement would have caught it. Second, the interval
performed least well in precisely the group at highest risk - those with the
most reduced renal function - because in that group accumulation proceeds
faster than the interval assumes.

The Committee's judgement is that the interval published in March is too long
for this population.

## 2. Revised requirement

For patients receiving CardioZen who have a recorded diagnosis of chronic
kidney disease, serum potassium must be checked every 60 days for the duration
of therapy.

This replaces the three-month interval published in SAFETY_DOC_007 section 3.
It takes effect on the date of this memorandum.

## 3. What this means in practice

Patients whose most recent potassium measurement falls within the new interval
require no immediate action; they move onto the shorter schedule from their
next measurement. Patients whose most recent measurement is older than the new
interval should have one arranged.

The Committee wishes to be explicit on one point: this revision is not a
statement that previously compliant care was substandard. It is a change to a
threshold in light of new operating data, and it should be communicated to
patients in those terms if the question arises.

## 4. Scope unchanged

The population remains as defined in SAFETY_DOC_007 - CardioZen recipients
with a diagnosis of chronic kidney disease. This memorandum makes no change
for patients without that diagnosis, and introduces no requirement for any
test other than serum potassium.

## 5. Status of SAFETY_DOC_007

SAFETY_DOC_007 should be treated as superseded in respect of the monitoring
interval in its section 3. Its remaining sections stand.
