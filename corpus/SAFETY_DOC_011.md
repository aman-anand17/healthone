# NOTICE TO PRESCRIBERS - PRESENTATION AND LABELLING

**Reference:** SAFETY_DOC_011
**Date of issue:** 18 August 2026
**Publisher:** Regional Medicines Safety Committee
**Distribution:** All prescribing sites; pharmacy

> **SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT - NOT REAL MEDICAL GUIDANCE.** Fabricated for a
> software demonstration. Hepatrol is an invented medication. Nothing here
> describes a real product or should inform any clinical decision.

## 1. Purpose

This notice concerns the presentation and carton labelling of Hepatrol. It
contains no change to any monitoring requirement and no new safety
information. It is circulated for pharmacy awareness only.

## 2. Change of presentation

The 40 mg tablet moves from a blister presentation to a bottle of sixty with
a desiccant. The active substance, strength and shelf life are unchanged.
Sites should exhaust existing blister stock before dispensing the new
presentation.

## 3. Carton labelling

The revised carton carries the ALT monitoring reminder previously printed on
the patient information leaflet only. The reminder text is a restatement of
the requirement already in force under SAFETY_DOC_006 and does not create a
second requirement. No change to the interval is intended or implied by this
notice.

## 4. Reporting

Suspected quality defects with the new presentation should be reported
through the usual site channel. There is no change to adverse-event
reporting.

## 5. Contact

Queries relating to this notice should be directed to the Committee
secretariat through the usual site channel.
