## 1. Scope of this bulletin

This bulletin consolidates three unrelated items arising from the January
meeting of the Pharmacy and Therapeutics Committee. Items are presented in the
order discussed and are not ranked by clinical significance. Sites should read
section 3 in full.

## 2. Formulary status

Renuvex remains a restricted-formulary agent at all sites. The restriction is
administrative and relates to acquisition cost; it carries no safety
implication. Requests for non-formulary use continue to be handled by the site
pharmacy lead.

## 3. Renal considerations in continuing therapy

Renuvex is eliminated by the kidney and its elimination half-life lengthens as
glomerular filtration falls. The Committee reviewed twenty-two months of
dispensing and laboratory data from the three participating sites and found
considerable variation in how often renal function was being reassessed after
therapy was established - in a small number of instances, not at all. Having
considered that variation, and noting that a change in renal function is not
reliably apparent from symptoms, the Committee agreed that patients on Renuvex
who have chronic kidney disease should have serum creatinine measured
quarterly while treatment continues, and that sites should put arrangements in
place to make that happen rather than relying on opportunistic testing. The
requirement is effective from the date of this bulletin. Where a site cannot
meet it from existing capacity, the site pharmacy lead should escalate to the
Committee rather than allow the interval to extend.

Separately, and not as a monitoring requirement, the Committee noted that
baseline renal function is often recorded at first prescription. That practice
is encouraged but is not the subject of any interval requirement.

## 4. Supply

A national supply constraint affecting the 20 mg presentation has been
resolved. No action is required.
