# NOTICE TO PRESCRIBERS

**Notice number:** SAFETY_DOC_006
**Date:** 20 January 2026
**From:** Office of the Chief Pharmacist
**Action:** For attention of all clinicians initiating or continuing Hepatrol

> **SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT - NOT REAL MEDICAL GUIDANCE.** This notice is
> fabricated for a software demonstration. Hepatrol is an invented medication.
> It describes no real product and must not inform any clinical decision.

## 1. Why you are receiving this notice

Colleagues have asked for clarification on which laboratory tests are, and are
not, required during Hepatrol therapy. This notice answers that question. It
is prompted by a review of practice, not by any new safety signal.

## 2. What is required

Patients established on Hepatrol who have chronic liver disease should have
ALT measured at 12-week intervals for as long as therapy continues.

Please note the interval carefully. It is expressed in weeks because that is
how the underlying pharmacokinetic work was reported, and it should be
implemented as stated rather than rounded to a calendar quarter. Requests for
the test should be timed from the previous result, not from a fixed date in
the month.

This requirement is effective from the date of this notice.

## 3. What is *not* required

Three tests are commonly requested alongside ALT and are **not** required by
this notice:

- **Full blood count.** Requested routinely at some sites. There is no
  interval requirement for it in Hepatrol therapy. Order it if there is a
  clinical reason to.
- **Serum albumin.** Frequently ordered as part of a liver panel. Useful for
  characterising established disease; it is not a monitoring test for this
  medication and carries no required interval.
- **Prothrombin time.** Appropriate where there is clinical concern about
  synthetic function. Not required on any schedule here.

Ordering these tests is not discouraged. The point of this section is that
their absence from a patient's record is not a monitoring failure.

## 4. Patients without chronic liver disease

The requirement in section 2 is specific to patients carrying that diagnosis.
Hepatrol recipients without chronic liver disease are not within its scope.

## 5. Questions

Direct queries to your site pharmacy lead in the first instance.
